Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Pixel Island ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information we collect

1. Information you provide directly

  • Account information: the App automatically creates an anonymous account identifier (a random ID tied to your iCloud account) — no sign-in, name or email is required
  • Photos you submit: images you choose for AI pet generation are sent to our processing service for the sole purpose of creating your pixel pet; the original photo is not stored on our servers after processing
  • Generated pets: the pixel-art pets created for you are stored on your account so they sync across your devices
  • Preferences: favorites and pet names you set
  • Messages to us (contact and feature-idea form): see "Contact and feature-idea form" below. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send

2. Information automatically collected

  • Purchase information: subscription and purchase status, processed through RevenueCat and Apple's payment system — we never see your payment details
  • Usage records: AI generation events (for gem accounting and fraud and abuse prevention), including technical metadata such as request time and outcome
  • Generation results (temporary): so that a creation interrupted by a network loss can be recovered without charging you twice, the result of a generation (the pixel pet image) may be kept on our servers for up to 24 hours, then it is deleted automatically
  • Device information: basic technical information needed to deliver the service (app version, IP address for rate limiting)
  • Usage analytics: in-app events (screens viewed, features used) associated with your anonymous account identifier, collected via PostHog to understand how the App is used and improve it
  • Session replay: for about half of installs, Pixel Island records the screens you see (not your photos or what you type: all images and text fields are masked) to fix usability problems. You can turn this off with "Share usage analytics" in Settings (see "Your choices on analytics" below).
  • Optional source question: if you answer "How did you hear about Pixel Island?", we keep your answer to learn which channels bring people, and share it with our subscription provider (RevenueCat).
  • Apple Ads attribution: if you came from one of our Apple Ads, the app asks Apple for an anonymous attribution token that says which campaign brought the install, never who you are.
  • Crash & performance data: crash reports and performance traces (device model, OS version, app version — no personal content), collected via Sentry to fix bugs
  • Weather (optional): if you enable the weather feature, your device location is used to fetch local weather through Apple WeatherKit, or through Open-Meteo when WeatherKit is unavailable (your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo only to get the forecast); coordinates are never stored on our servers
  • Music (optional): if you use the iPod Music Widget and allow access to Apple Music, the App reads the track you are playing and its artwork on your device to display them. To show lyrics, the track title and artist (no account or device identifier) are sent to LRCLIB, a public lyrics service. Nothing about your music is stored on our servers

3. Information stored only on your device

  • Selected pet, widget configurations and display preferences
  • Local copies of your pet collection
  • Your last location fix for the weather widget (if enabled)
  • Photos you choose for the photo widget (if you use it)

Contact and feature-idea form

The App has a form to contact us or suggest a feature. When you tap Send, we receive:

  • your message;
  • your email address, only if you choose to enter one (so we can reply);
  • the technical information listed in the form, which you can expand and read before sending: device model, iOS and App version, settings such as battery level, Low Power Mode, dark mode, text size, language and region, free disk space, network type (Wi-Fi or cellular, online or offline), whether some audio is playing (never what), the state of the App (plan, gem balance, number of pets, widgets and Live Activities, notification permission), and technical identifiers of your account, analytics and subscription records.

It does not include your photos, contacts, location or the content of your music. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

Purpose: to answer you and to understand and fix the problem you describe. Storage: your message is saved in our backend database and emailed to our support inbox. A limit on the number of messages per account protects the form from abuse. Retention: we keep it for up to 24 months, then delete it, or earlier if you ask us to (contact us below).

Fraud and abuse prevention

The AI generation service costs us real money per request, so we protect it from abuse (for example, creating many accounts to get free credits, or fraudulent purchases). For this we process your account identifier, the history of when accounts were created, your purchases and our server logs (such as request time, outcome and IP address for rate limiting). This happens whether or not you share usage analytics. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of paid AI credits (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and you can object to it as described in "Your rights".

Your choices on analytics

The Share usage analytics switch in Settings controls product analytics and session replay (PostHog) only. When it is off, the App stops sending those events and recordings. It does not turn off the data described under "Fraud and abuse prevention", purchase processing, the temporary generation results, crash and performance reports (Sentry), or a message you choose to send us.

How we use your information

  • Service delivery: to generate your AI pets, sync your collection, and display your gem balance
  • Subscription management: to unlock Premium features and credit gems you purchased
  • Fraud and abuse prevention: to enforce fair usage limits on the AI generation service and prevent fraudulent use of paid credits
  • Customer support: to respond to your messages and fix the problems you report
  • App improvement: to fix bugs and improve features

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your photos or generated pets to train AI models.

Pets you mark as favorites may be built into a future App update so they animate in the Dynamic Island without the App running; they remain tied to your account and are not shown to other users.

Third-party services

  • Apple — payments, App Store, iCloud, WeatherKit. Privacy policy
  • Supabase — account database and authentication, and storage of contact-form messages and temporary generation results (hosted in the EU). Privacy policy
  • Google Gemini API — AI image processing for pet generation; photos are processed transiently. Privacy policy
  • Cloudflare — secure proxy in front of our AI service, including per-IP rate limiting, and delivery of contact-form messages to our support inbox by email. Privacy policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. Privacy policy
  • PostHog — product analytics (hosted in the US). Privacy policy
  • Sentry — crash reporting and performance monitoring. Privacy policy
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecast when Apple WeatherKit is unavailable (weather feature only). Terms & privacy
  • LRCLIB — song lyrics for the iPod Music Widget (track title and artist only). Website

Data retention and deletion

Your account data (profile, gem balance, generated pets, purchase records) is retained while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time from Settings → Erase my data in the App. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Generation results kept for crash recovery are deleted after 24 hours at most. Messages sent through the contact form are kept for up to 24 months (see above); to have one deleted sooner, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on your location (including under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise these rights via the in-app deletion feature or by contacting us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Authentication tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. Server-side access to your data is restricted by row-level security so that only your authenticated account can read it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated by updating the "Last updated" date above.

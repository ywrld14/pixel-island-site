Quick answers

How do I restore my purchases?
  1. Open Pixel Island on a device signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.
  2. Go to Settings › Restore purchases (or tap Restore in the Shop).
  3. Your Premium comes back within a few seconds.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by the app. On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Pixel Island › Cancel Subscription. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial or current period ends. You keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.
How do I turn on Live Activities (the pet in the Dynamic Island)?
  1. Open the iPhone Settings app › Pixel Island › turn on Live Activities.
  2. In the app, also check that the Live Activities switch in Settings is on.
  3. Leave the app: the Dynamic Island doesn't show your pet while the app is in the foreground.

If needed, also check Settings › Face ID & Passcode › Live Activities.

How do I add a widget?
  1. Long-press an empty spot on your Home Screen, then tap + (or Edit › Add Widget).
  2. Search for Pixel Island, pick a size and tap Add Widget.
  3. Long-press the widget › Edit Widget to pick a theme (Classic, Casino Slots, iPod Music Widget...).

For the Lock Screen: long-press your Lock Screen › Customize › add a Pixel Island widget.

My pet doesn't appear in the Dynamic Island
The Dynamic Island hides Live Activities while the app is in the foreground: go home or open another app to see your pet. Also check that Live Activities are on (see above). Pets live in the Dynamic Island on iPhone 14 Pro and newer; on other models they show up in the Lock Screen banner.
My widget shows a lock
Widgets are a Premium feature. Unlock Premium in the app (Shop tab) and the widget unlocks by itself within a few seconds.
My widget disappeared after the update
Pixel Island now has a single widget with themes (Classic, Casino Slots, iPod Music Widget). Long-press your Home Screen › Edit › Add Widget › Pixel Island, then long-press the widget › Edit Widget to pick a theme.
My gems didn't arrive after purchase
Balances update within a few seconds of a purchase. If they don't, force-quit and reopen the app; if the problem persists, email us with your purchase receipt.
How do I delete my account and data?
In the app: Settings › Erase my data. This permanently removes your cloud data, pets and gems.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is Pixel Island free?
The app is free to download, with a new free pet every day. Premium unlocks every pet, scene and widget, with a 7-day free trial. You can cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.
Which iPhones does it work on?
On iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Pets live in the Dynamic Island on models that have one (iPhone 14 Pro and newer); on other iPhones your pet shows up in the Lock Screen banner. Widgets work on all of them.
My pet doesn't show in the Dynamic Island

Make sure Live Activities are on: Settings › Pixel Island › Live Activities. The Dynamic Island doesn't show the activity while the app is in the foreground, so go home or open another app.

Still stuck? Head to the Support page.

What happens to my photo?
It's sent to our AI service only to create your pet, and isn't kept on our servers afterwards. It isn't used to train AI models. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
Do I need Apple Music?
Only the iPod-style music widget uses Apple Music, and it asks for your permission first. Everything else works without it.
How do I get in touch?
In the app: Settings › Contact us. Or email pixel@refresh-agency.fr. You'll also find answers on the Support page.

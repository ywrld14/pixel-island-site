FAQ

Quick answers

Is Pixel Island free?
Yes. The app is free, with 2 new free pets every day, the Tamago console and the golden Casino. Premium unlocks everything, with a free trial. You can cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.
Which iPhones does it work on?
On iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Pets live in the Dynamic Island on models that have one (iPhone 14 Pro and newer); on other iPhones your pet shows up in the Lock Screen banner. Widgets work on all of them.
My pet doesn't show in the Dynamic Island

Make sure Live Activities are on: Settings › Pixel Island › Live Activities. The island doesn't show the activity while the app is open, so go back to your Home Screen.

Still stuck? Head to the Support page.

What happens to my photo?
It's sent to our AI service only to create your pet, and isn't kept on our servers afterwards. It isn't used to train AI models. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
Do I need Apple Music?
Only the music widgets (iPod, disc, lyrics) use Apple Music, and they ask for your permission first. Everything else works without it.
Which languages?
English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian and Turkish.
How do I get in touch?
In the app: Settings › Contact us. Or email pixel@refresh-agency.fr. More answers on the Support page.
